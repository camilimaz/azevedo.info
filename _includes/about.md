**Welcome!**

I am an Associate Professor at [DTU Management](http://www.transportmodeling.man.dtu.dk), [Technical University of Denmark](http://www.dtu.dk). My main research interests are the mathematical modeling and simulation of human mobility, smart mobility services and the modeling, development and assessment of new technologies in transportation systems.

I am also a Research Affiliate at the [ITSLab](http://its.mit.edu) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)](http://www.mit.edu/). Before joining DTU, I was a Research Scientist at the [ITSLab](http://its.mit.edu) and the Executive Director of the [Transportation Education Committee](https://cee.mit.edu/graduate/graduate-degree/) at [MIT](http://www.mit.edu/). My early career started as a research scholar at [LNEC (Portugal)](http://www.lnec.pt/en/lnec/presentation/) and then as a Senior PostDoctoral Associate at [SMART (Singapore)](http://smart.mit.edu/).
