**Welcome!**

I am an Associate Professor at [DTU Management](https://www.man.dtu.dk), [Technical University of Denmark](https://www.dtu.dk), and Head of Studies of the [MSc in Transport and Logistics](https://www.dtu.dk/english/education/graduate/msc-programmes/transport-and-logistics). My research focuses on the mathematical modelling and simulation of human behaviour in transportation, smart mobility services, and the modelling, development and assessment of new technologies in mobility systems.

I am also a Research Affiliate at the [ITSLab](http://its.mit.edu) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)](http://www.mit.edu/). Before joining DTU, I was a Research Scientist at the [ITSLab](http://its.mit.edu) and the Executive Director of the [Transportation Education Committee](https://cee.mit.edu/graduate/graduate-degree/) at [MIT](http://www.mit.edu/). My early career started as a research scholar at [LNEC (Portugal)](http://www.lnec.pt/en/) and then as a Senior Postdoctoral Associate at [SMART (Singapore)](http://smart.mit.edu/).
